GIS ADMINISTRATION & DEVELOPMENT

Building reliable GIS systems, data, and workflows.

As GIS Administrator for Warren County, Virginia, I manage and maintain the organization's GIS environment, including its spatial databases, services, applications, and supporting workflows. I have modernized major parts of the environment through database schema updates, workflow automation, database-platform changes, ArcGIS Server upgrades, and the transition from ArcMap-based publishing to ArcGIS Pro and a modern enterprise GIS architecture.

My work has included leading the GIS portion of integrating local data into a web-based Computer Aided Dispatch environment and developing a complex SQL query to retrieve selected real estate information from the Vision database for integration with parcel data. These projects reflect the type of work I enjoy most: connecting systems, improving repeatability, and building products around a real operational need.

In December 2022, I graduated from James Madison University with a Bachelor of Science in Geographic Science and concentrations in Environmental Conservation, Sustainability, and Development (ECSD) and Applied Geographic Information Systems (AGIS). While in college, I returned to a previous employer as an unpaid intern, where I assisted the GIS Technician and gained practical, real-world GIS experience. That experience helped position me for a local-government role with the County of Roanoke as a GIS Specialist in Development Services, where my work focused on GIS utility data input and maintenance.

I enjoy the challenge of producing reliable, reusable products that make other people's work easier. I am especially passionate about maintaining accurate data for the public and for emergency-response agencies. My goal is to continue expanding my programming, automation, and enterprise GIS skills and eventually provide practical GIS solutions to clients seeking more efficient, maintainable, and dependable GIS environments.

SELECTED WORK

Proven Projects

Examples of GIS modernization, automation, integration, and public-facing work I have delivered.

ENTERPRISE GIS

ArcMap to ArcGIS Pro & Enterprise Modernization

Upgraded a locality's ArcGIS Server environment from 10.4 to 11.5, deployed a full in-house enterprise GIS build, transitioned publishing workflows from ArcMap to ArcGIS Pro, and migrated the enterprise database platform from Microsoft SQL Server to PostgreSQL.

NG9-1-1

NG9-1-1 Data Upload Automation

Developed an adaptable Python parsing and transformation workflow that can ingest varied in-house schemas and produce data structured for National Emergency Number Association (NENA) requirements and NG9-1-1 upload workflows such as Intrado EsiNet.

PUBLIC SAFETY

Computer Aided Dispatch Migration

Developed a Python workflow to extract selected layers from the in-house GIS environment and package them in the format required by GIS-driven Computer Aided Dispatch software, simplifying repeatable data delivery to the dispatching system.

SQL & INTEGRATION

Real Estate SQL Integration

Developed a complex SQL query to retrieve selected real estate attributes from a locality database and join them to parcel features through a shared key field. The query is designed so requested fields can be added or removed as requirements change.

WEB GIS

Cloud-Hosted Parcel Viewer

Built a public parcel viewer with ArcGIS Experience Builder in ArcGIS Online and developed a Python-assisted weekly update workflow to accommodate network and infrastructure constraints. The application operates at fewer than 130 ArcGIS Online credits per month.

PYTHON AUTOMATION

Official Zoning Map Book

Developed a Python workflow to refresh more than 300 ArcGIS Pro projects, access their active layouts, export the required map pages, and support generation of an official locality zoning map book.

PUBLIC ACCESS

ArcGIS Hub Implementation

Developed an ArcGIS Hub and ArcGIS Online landing experience, incorporating HTML and CSS to give the public a clearer and more accessible entry point to locality GIS applications, maps, and resources.
