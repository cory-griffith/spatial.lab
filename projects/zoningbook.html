Official Zoning Map Book

Developed a Python workflow to refresh more than 300 ArcGIS Pro projects, access their active layouts, export the required map pages, and support generation of an official locality zoning map book.

Cartography Python Zoning

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Why this was necessary

The development of a color-coded digital zoning map book provides a quick and reliable reference for locating and understanding zoning throughout the locality. Organized by tax map number, the map book combines tax maps, inserts, zoning classifications, and historical plat research for isolated or unique areas into one accessible resource.

This resource supports both internal staff and external users by simplifying property research, improving access to zoning information, and reducing the time needed to locate and understand specific areas of interest.

WORKFLOW

How it happened.

  1. Research & Preparation.
    Research the existing tax map numbering system and historical records to identify and properly reference each area of interest.
  2. Develop a Standard Template.
    Create a standardized ArcGIS Pro (APRX) template and document formatting, symbology, naming, and layout standards for consistency across all map projects.
  3. Isolate Areas of Interest.
    Develop individual map projects based on each specific tax map, insert, or area of interest.
  4. Automate Map Exports.
    Use a developed Python script to automatically process and export the completed layouts into the final digital map book.
Design note:Maintain uniform project structure, symbology, naming conventions, and layout formatting throughout the workflow to ensure consistency and organization as the map book expands.
