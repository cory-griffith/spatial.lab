PROJECT OVERVIEW

Why this was necessary

Quality control of parcels, address points, and road centerlines is essential because these datasets support property identification, navigation, emergency response, and numerous other GIS applications. Regular QC helps identify geometry, attribution, addressing, and connectivity errors before they affect downstream systems or users.

Outcome

These scripts essentially help me maintain a very large dataset. These identify problems areas & improve data accuracy. The idea is to conintue to build out data quality scripts to ensure everything is in good shape. The scripts are designed to be run on a regular basis to catch any issues that may arise over time. This helps identify issues within the real estate database too, which is a huge benefit to the organization.
